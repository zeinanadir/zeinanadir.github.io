I'm a writer and journalist focused on indigenous resistance, im(migration) and women's health. Before joining the University of Virginia to cover health and climate research, I reported on maternal/reproductive health and environmental justice at the Boston Globe. Prior to that, I covered indigenous resistance movements, climate protest and education inequity at POLITICO’s E&E News.

An incoming PhD at Rutgers University's Geography Department, my academic research examines how communities preserve cultural practices amidst mass displacement, foreign land grabs and border militarization, with a focus on Sudan and the wider Sahara.
