I'm a journalist/writer covering environmental and human health. Before joining the University of Virginia, I reported on maternal/reproductive health and climate justice at the Boston Globe. Prior to that, I covered climate movements, treaty violations and education inequity at POLITICO.

Next year, I begin a PhD in (human) geography at Rutgers University to study cultural traditions in Sudan and the wider Sahel/Red Sea regions. As these regions sustain the largest ongoing displacement crises, I archive how communities experience and resist mass migration, (inter)state violence and border militarization.
